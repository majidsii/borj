# Decision — Settings entry point and unbuilt sections in the shell (2026-11-08)

**Status:** approved by the owner, 2026-11-08: «اره پیش برو ولی اونارو هم باید پیاده کنیم».
**Deviates from:** the boards in `docs/superpowers/references/2026-11-05-glass-mockups/` (which win on layout per redesign spec §1).

## 1. Settings entry (no board shows one)

- Phone: a glass circle settings button in the Today toolbar, next to the calendar button.
- Desktop and medium: a «تنظیمات» row at the bottom of the sidebar (above the backup status line), plus the `Ctrl+,` shortcut.

## 2. Sections whose feature does not exist yet

The boards show عادت‌ها (phone tab, sidebar), هدف‌ها and بودجه (sidebar). Until each feature exists, its tab or row is hidden; the phone tab bar has three tabs (امروز / کارها / مالی) plus the search circle. When a feature ships, its tab or row returns as on the boards.

The owner added that these must be built («اونارو هم باید پیاده کنیم»): habits, goals and budgets are required product scope, not optional. Habits and goals are roadmap §0 item 4; budgets is added to the same program item. Each needs its own spec (redesign spec §7, "out of scope here").
